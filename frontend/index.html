    Questions

    Do I need an account to order?

    Browsing is open. Checkout needs an account, which you create with your phone number and a password.

    Which kitchen am I ordering from?

    Each order is placed with a single kitchen. Your cart and checkout both name it at the top.

    How does the kitchen know when I'll arrive?

    Put it in the note for the kitchen at checkout, for example "arriving around 1:15". The order's cook time is shown in your cart so you can plan the walk.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Request cancellation from the order's tracking page before the kitchen starts cooking. The kitchen confirms it, and the page tells you when it has.

    Where do I see my past orders and points?

    Orders lists every order with its status. Rewards shows your balance, tier and history for each kitchen.
